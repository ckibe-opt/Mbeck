# myShop User Manual

Welcome to myShop! This guide will help you, the shop owner, use the myShop app to manage your business effectively.

## 1. Getting Started: Your First Steps

### 1.1. Adding a New Product to Your Inventory
Your inventory is the heart of your shop. Here’s how to add a new item:

1.  Go to the **Inventory** screen.
2.  Tap the **'+'** or **"Add New Item"** button.
3.  **Take a Picture:** Capture a clear, top-down photo of the product. This is crucial for the AI to recognize it later.
4.  **Enter Details:**
    *   **Product Name:** (e.g., "Dasani Water 500ml")
    *   **Buying Price:** The price you paid for the item.
    *   **Selling Price:** The price your customers will pay.
5.  **Save** the item.

> 💡 Pro Tip: For better AI accuracy, tap **"Train AI with new Angle"** after saving. Take photos of the product from the side or back. This helps the AI recognize it from different perspectives.

### 1.2. Making a Sale (Quick Sell)
The AI scanner makes selling fast and easy.

1.  From the **Home Screen**, open the **Scanner** by clicking the **Quick Sell** button.
2.  Point your phone's camera at the product you want to sell.
3.  A list of the top 5 matches will appear on the screen.
4.  Tap the correct item from the list.
5.  **Apply Discount (Optional):** If you want to give a discount, enter it.
6.  Tap **"Proceed"** to complete the sale.

## 2. Daily Operations

### 2.1. Managing Your Customers
Keep track of your customers for better service.

1.  Go to the **Customers** screen.
2.  Tap **"Add New Customer"** to save a customer's `Name`, `Phone Number`, and `ID`.
3.  **Importing Customers:** You can import a list of customers from a CSV file using the **Import** feature.

### 2.2. Daily Closing: Balancing Your Books
At the end of each day, it's important to make sure your money is balanced.

1.  **Count Your Cash:** Use the **"Cash Count"** tool on the Home Screen. It has a calculator to help you count all your physical notes and coins quickly.
2.  **Save to Accounting:** Once you have the total, save it to the Accounting module.
3.  **Enter Digital Balances:** Go to the **Accounting** screen and enter your current balances for M-Pesa, Equity, Co-op, or any other digital account.
4.  **Review the Disparity Report:** The app will automatically compare the money you *should* have (based on sales) with the money you *actually* counted.
    *   **Balanced:** Great! Everything matches.
    *   **Surplus:** You have more money than expected.
    *   **Shortage:** You have less money than expected. This helps you find issues quickly.

## 3. Security and Reports

### 3.1. How to Verify a Customer's Payment
Some customers may show a fake "Payment Successful" message. myShop protects you from this.

1.  When you complete a sale, the app generates a unique **Digital Receipt** with a `Ref Code` and `Signature`.
2.  If a customer claims they paid via a message, ask to see their receipt details.
3.  On the **Home Screen**, tap the **"Verify Receipt"** tool.
4.  Enter the `Ref Code` and `Signature` from the customer's receipt.
5.  The app will instantly tell you if the receipt is **Valid** and exists in your secure sales log. If it's not found, the receipt is not legitimate.

### 3.2. Understanding Your Business with Reports
Knowledge is power. Use reports to see how your business is doing.

*   **Profits Screen:** See which items are your **most profitable**, not just the ones you sell the most.
*   **Reports Screen:**
    *   See your **Income vs. Expenses** for the week, month, or year.
    *   View leaderboards of your **best-selling items**.
    *   Analyze restock needs to ensure you never run out of popular products.

## 4. Backing Up Your Data
Your data is safe, even if you change phones.

*   Go to **Settings** and use the **"Full Backup"** feature.
*   This creates a secure file with all your sales, inventory, and product images.
*   You can use this file to restore your shop's data on a new device.
